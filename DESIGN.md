# Design System: Alchemist's Descent

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Living Descent"**

The Living Descent is a wet, corroded alchemical refinery. Slate, mineral deposits, timber and copper carry clustered pixel detail; warm refuge light and pale living bodies establish local points of attention. Broken terrain faces, attached foliage and species-specific anatomy give the inhabited foreground its texture. Visible joints, missing limbs, bent crowns and falling vine fragments follow their physical state. Water, terrain, creatures and working machinery remain independent, mutable parts of the game. Distant refinery imagery provides atmosphere and depth.

The interface uses thin controls, warm pale lettering and quiet corners around the scene. Cormorant Garamond gives entry, room, rest and death headings their character; system text carries explanations and ordinary controls. A lower slate/brass death panel preserves the visible ivory-and-copper body. Optional Trickshot cues add native mint/brass targeting and brief combat readouts within the same scene. The separate offline capture library uses slate and brass with system-sans headings so the media remains prominent. The boot screen and authoring workshops keep their existing identity; spell-card details and shop controls retain some inherited treatments.

Source authority is [Living Descent CSS](src/styles/living-descent.css), its inherited [base CSS](src/styles/main.css), [entry markup](index.html), the [capture-library generator](scripts/screenshot-gallery.mjs), and the rendering and component sources below. The [surface brief](.impeccable/surfaces/src-game-game-ts.md) owns this expedition's composition; the [quality bar](.impeccable/quality-bar.md) and [asset manifest](docs/living-descent-asset-manifest.md) record the material direction. Generated compositions remain nonliteral references. Bounded art, Weaver/gallery and environment reviews are closed; the Trickshot/death extension has a SHIP verdict with its tuning-text and compact-panel findings resolved. Waterfall and held-limb corrections also have bounded SHIP verdicts. Source, reviewed stills and disclosed fixture measurements support this extraction within those reviews' limits. Subjective motion, listening, unaided play and hardware performance need their own evidence. [Fidelity and physical response](docs/living-descent-fidelity-and-physics.md) and [Trickshot combat](docs/trickshot-combat.md) hold implementation and validation evidence, including the separate worldgen and serial-timing gates; gameplay architecture remains in [docs/DESIGN.md](docs/DESIGN.md).

**Key Characteristics:**

- Clustered wet stone, worn copper, connected timber grain and material-rooted foliage.
- Live material boundaries and detailed articulated bodies above subdued distant scenery.
- Continuous anatomical attachments, visible limb loss and foliage that travels with its support.
- Momentum carries water over ledges and swings a held limb's free thigh from its driven shank.
- Species-specific plates, membranes, gills, scutes and state-driven faces at the established actor scale.
- Serif titles paired with plain operating text.
- Mostly square, lightly bordered controls, small rest-panel corner exceptions and sparse corner HUD groups.
- Contextual input cues, independent flask slots and captions that follow their objective.
- Optional first-contact aiming cues and a death composition that keeps the physical body visible.
- A quiet capture library that discloses pose fixtures and keeps historical media findable.

**Display Font:** locally hosted Cormorant Garamond, with Georgia and serif fallbacks. The font face supports weights 300–700 and uses `font-display: swap`.

**The Title and Instruction Rule.** Cormorant carries entry, room, pause, settings, rest and death headings. Explanations and ordinary controls use their defined system-sans roles; flask keys and equipment annotations retain their source monospace treatment.

Text-size preferences are Standard (1), Large (1.15) and Larger (1.3), applied through `--text-scale`. The source explicitly scales operating labels, settings, captions, boon descriptions, Descend and flask widths. Entry, pause and rest display headings keep their own size rules; this is not a global zoom operation. At the compact breakpoint, the entry display becomes 64px and boon names 20px. The four Trickshot tuning labels and four numeric outputs inherit the scaled tuning role; the supplied Larger-text captures measure all eight at 16.9px. Death stats, cause and Return also scale. The short combat readout remains 13px, and the death heading follows its own clamp and short-height override.

The backing canvas remains 1280 × 720. The `pixelScale=1` startup comparison and CPU fallback use classic one-cell detail; the experimental, query-gated WebGPU diagnostic is configured for that density. This is presentation density, not a larger simulation or a camera zoom. Source: [presentation](src/render/presentation.ts), [constants](src/config/constants.ts), [FrameComposer](src/render/FrameComposer.ts) and [ComposeShader](src/render/ComposeShader.ts).

## Elevation & Depth

The game plane supplies depth through separate refinery layers, cell-bound surface detail, boundary lighting, articulated silhouettes and actual material motion. Default distant-refinery parallax is 0.1 at full opacity; copper machinery is 0.15 at 0.52 opacity, with both raster scales set to 0.5 in [backdrop settings](src/config/backdrop.ts). Their production pixel dimensions are asset facts, independent of the current logical viewport.

Entry artwork receives a left-to-right slate veil behind text. Play HUD tracks, slots, prompts and the canvas frame remove their inherited box shadows. Pause retains a 2px backdrop blur and sanctum 4px; the native settings dialog darkens its backdrop. Death uses a transparent upper field and a restrained bottom gradient with no backdrop blur, keeping the physical body visible. Small text shadows support field notes and sound captions.
