# Sprite sheet prompts (gpt-image-2, 3:2, 2K, high)

Provenance for `public/assets/arena/fighters/*/sprites.png`. The reference images are wired in this order: portrait,
animation sheet, then (prototype fighters only) the fighter's rows of core-attacks, motion-defense and ledge-movement; sheet
B also takes the chosen sheet A. Other fighters use the same text with their own CHARACTER paragraph and weapon-specific
attack descriptions.

## Ilyra Voss, sheet A (locomotion)

Game asset: a pixel-art SPRITE SHEET for a 2D side-view platform fighter. The frames will be cut out and used directly as in-game animation.

CHARACTER: Ilyra Voss, exactly as shown in the reference images. The first reference is her official portrait, the second is her animation reference sheet, and the third, fourth and fifth are approved concept rows showing her attacks, dodges and ledge moves. Keep her identity identical: wide-brimmed cream hat, long pale braided hair, teal long coat with brass trim, dark trousers, brown boots, brass pistol-wand in her right hand, belt pouches and small glowing flasks. Same palette, same proportions and the same pixel-art rendering as the concept rows: crisp dark one-pixel outline, three to four tone shading, warm brass highlights.

LAYOUT: a strict grid of 6 columns by 3 rows of equal cells on a solid, flat, pure magenta background (#FF00FF) that fills the entire image. Exactly one full-body sprite per cell, centred horizontally, feet resting on the same baseline near the bottom of each cell. All sprites at the SAME scale: the standing pose is about 65% of the cell height. Every sprite faces RIGHT. No text, no labels, no numbers, no grid lines, no borders, no floor, no shadows, no motion echoes, no muzzle flash, no magic effects, no particles: only the character on magenta.

ROW 1: (1) idle ready stance, wand lowered; (2) idle, breathing in, shoulders slightly raised; (3) idle, breathing out; (4) idle, small weight shift; (5) landing crouch, knees bent, coat settling; (6) recovery: rising upward with the wand arm thrust straight overhead, coat trailing below.
ROW 2: a six-frame side-view RUN cycle in order: contact, down, passing, up, contact with the other leg, passing. Coat tails flare behind her.
ROW 3: (1) jump take-off, rising, knees tucked; (2) jump apex, legs bent; (3) falling, arms up for balance, coat lifting; (4) fast-fall: compact, knees tucked, plummeting; (5) hurt recoil, head and torso snapped back; (6) launched tumble: body nearly horizontal, spinning, limbs flung.

STYLE: designed to read at small size in game: bold silhouette, big hat brim, clearly separated limbs. Chunky pixel art with clearly visible square pixels; the standing pose is about 60 art pixels tall.
