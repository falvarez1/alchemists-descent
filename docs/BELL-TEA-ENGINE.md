# The Unreasonable Bell & Tea Engine

The first level's required bell-making workshop occupies over 1,100 world cells above the main inspection catwalk. Follow the upper walkway right from the Intake and use the brass crank (E by default). The chain produces the bell required by the lower descent gate. Walking past the machine cannot clear the level.

The opening is a hands-on ignition followed by a physical demonstration: a thin powder trail releases a rope pendulum, its impact rolls a boulder downhill, and the boulder opens a finite water reservoir. The current carries a wooden duck as rising water lifts it to a trip. That releases acid onto a sacrificial stone pin. A falling sugar weight then opens the lava tap; lava quenches against water in the kettle and actual steam lifts the piston. The piston opens oil and strikes the final fuse. Three wooden powder casings detonate in sequence around an absurdly small tea cup, releasing the bell at the receiver.

The director observes heat, surviving pin cells, body positions, water contact and spent powder. It only moves mechanical stops and valves, plus the two crank/piston sparkers. It never advances on a timer or creates an ongoing material supply. If a player sabotages the engine, a 40-second stage timeout returns control. Using its crank recharges only the workshop rectangle; damage to the catwalk and the rest of the level persists. Repair creates no harvestable gold.

During the action camera, player movement, damage, attacks and flask input are suspended. The simulation continues over the entire workshop. Framing follows live moving bodies or the burning fuse, easing between close views and wider handoffs. Esc, the visible Return to player button, or controller B returns control while the chain keeps running. Skipping the camera never bypasses the objective. Reduced camera motion disables the close zoom. Completion gives a short final hold, then eases back to the player.

Rapier ropes use fixed anchors and maximum-length joints. Heat and acid can sever their live spans. Floating rigid bodies respond to water currents; marked piston bodies gain bounded lift only from steam immediately underneath. These capabilities are available independently of this encounter. The bell uses the existing append-only key pickup ID and bell artwork.

Expedition saves retain stage, elapsed ticks and surviving prop poses, velocities and rope attachment. Reopening a save resumes physical work without forcing the camera back on. Level/mode transitions relinquish the action camera. Builder documents and raw Sandbox saves do not receive this expedition state. GEN_VERSION 41 records the intentional first-level geometry change; earlier-generation expeditions follow the existing retirement behavior.
